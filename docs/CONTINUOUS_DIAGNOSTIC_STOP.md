# Diagnostic scope decision — November 9, before the remaining generators

The two real-data candidates fail the frozen numerical gates under both evaluated
proposal methods. Process workers are active, not deadlocked, but full-design
fits take minutes per response; completing the 192 requested fits and independent
rebuild would consume several additional hours. The initial full-calibration
attempts are **incomplete**, not passing or fully evaluated studies.

This run will finish **eight diagnostic fits per generator**, indices 0–7, in
the original fixed order. These indices are not selected by coverage or football
results. Keep the same 64 requested draws, seed, target posterior, benchmark,
sampling cap, accuracy thresholds and interval definition. The new
`continuous-variance-diagnostic-v1.json` adds an execution cap only. Previously
examined draws are consumed numerical diagnostics, not fresh independent tests.
The off-grid and fixed-year generators have not been evaluated at this decision.

Every final diagnostic run recomputes its 24 fits from the original archive;
no saved checkpoint is selected as an input. The report must separate attempted,
numerically failed and unattempted trials. All unverified trials remain in the
**64-per-generator denominator** for coverage bounds. Success-only summaries,
if present, are explicitly selected diagnostics. They cannot certify nominal
coverage, erase the stopped study or admit a module. Full calibration remains
unfinished regardless of the diagnostic numbers. This is a compute/scope stop,
not a relaxed scientific gate or a statistical rejection of the continuous prior.

The next recovery unit must first establish a numerically reliable reference for
the weak team/game-variance tails. Preregister a staged pilot and compute budget
before another full calibration campaign. Preserve the original 64-draw plans,
all checkpoint stores, the no-admission decision and sealed Week 10 outcomes.
Do not add complexity or change variance priors in response to a favorable subset.

The interrupted executor also exposed slow shutdown of queued work. The final
bounded process pool uses its public context manager to terminate only its own
workers on interruption. Serial/process results and absence of leftover children
are regression-tested. The earlier remaining workers were identified by exact
parent/child command lines and terminated; their checkpoints were preserved.
