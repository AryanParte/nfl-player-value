## What the experiment tests

An importance proposal chooses where to spend integration work. Matching posterior
mass alone need not sample the regions that matter to a mean or tail probability.
This candidate fits three Gaussian components to mean/mass, lower-endpoint and
upper-endpoint envelopes, retaining the original 10% defensive component. Two
independent pilot rounds precede a separate zero-mean control fit and evaluation.
Each prior block supplies its own estimated normalizing mass. Full mixture
weights integrate the original target; signed player values are not clipped.

All eight cases request 1,024 independently fitted trials. Both full offline
executions use the same frozen seeds; the second is reconstruction evidence,
not another independent sample. Original five, added broad/displaced tails and
the new remote narrow component are retained, including failed confidence sets.
The paired plain estimator is diagnostic, never a post-result replacement.
Precision still requires discrepancy plus numerical allowance within 0.01 SD
for means and 0.001 for CDFs in at least 90% of trials. Numerical coverage checks
do not establish player uncertainty calibration or attribution validity.

Per archived case the unchanged accounting permits 88,065 target evaluations
against a 90,000 cap; the 1,800-second case and 7,200-second five-case caps remain.
These caps apply to the archived NFL pilot, not the much larger analytic coverage
study. No automatic sample enlargement, favorable restart, posterior jitter,

## Failure diagnosis

The broad-tail target 5 passes precision in 1,024/1,024 trials, versus 6.0547% in
the preceding package. Its joint numerical coverage is 95.8008%. This comparison
uses fresh draws and a package that reallocates coefficient-refit work to proposal
adaptation; it does not isolate one change in a paired factorial experiment.

Target 6 improves from 0% to **33.5938%** precision, still below the required 90%.
All 1,024 confidence constructions finish, and the finite coverage checks pass
(joint coverage 95.1172%). Every point-only mean and endpoint discrepancy is within
tolerance. Adding numerical uncertainty produces eight mean failures and 680
endpoint failures. The median maximum endpoint discrepancy plus allowance is
.00106482 CDF against .001; the median maximum mean total is .00665631 SD against
.01. The paired plain estimator's precision is 40.2344%, so it also fails. Its
selection after inspecting results would violate the frozen plan. Controlled
empirical variance is worse than plain for six of seven reported functionals;
no claim of general variance reduction follows from successful training fits.

Target 6's second-round normalizing-mass estimates span .995086–1.004535, with
median 1.000054. Its median component-mean distances from the fine-target population
reference are .00622, .01090 and .02055 latent-coordinate units; corresponding
covariance Frobenius gaps are .04991, .05533 and .12314. The fitted density-ratio
probe at (4,4,0,0) has median log ratio 1.78360 versus 1.78380 for the population
reference and 6.86489 initially. The fit is close to this moment approximation,
yet the required functional precision is not achieved. That makes larger pilots
alone an unsupported remedy. It does not prove an oracle version of the family
would fail: no oracle precision trial was conducted.

Target 7 exposes a larger problem: **0% precision and 30.9570% joint coverage**.
Forty-seven confidence constructions fail; all 1,024 point estimates remain
defined and retained in point-error diagnostics. Among the 977 constructed
confidence sets, every trial fails both mean and endpoint precision. Median
maximum discrepancy plus allowance is .307291 SD and .0251488 CDF, against .01
and .001. Point-only failures occur in 864/977 and 875/977 of that subset.
All-trial RMSE for the second standardized mean is .377809 SD; excluding failed
confidence constructions would conceal part of the failure. The plain estimator
also has 0% precision and only 31.0547% joint coverage.

The median first/second pilot mass estimates are .900145/.900390. By construction,
the coarse target's true mass lies between .999 and 1.001. The median fitted
log-density ratio at (8,8,0,0), 12.89185, is effectively unchanged from the initial
proposal, versus 3.25488 for the fine-target population reference. Median component
mean gaps from that reference are 1.73760, .69774 and 3.61230 latent-coordinate
units; covariance gaps are 24.9369, 11.3717 and 42.6832. Some fits are dominated by
individual pilot points (maximum normalized upper-envelope weight .991206 in the
second round). This supports a missed-mass/discovery diagnosis, distinct from
target 6's close fit to its moment approximation. It is not proof that an oracle
proposal would satisfy precision, or that every observed failure has one cause.

The separate initial-proposal diagnostic reaches the earlier NFL hierarchy's
.103866 maximum-replicate-share proxy in 8/32 trials; its maximum is .746012.
Thus the stronger concentration regime is observed in this declared diagnostic,
not merely inferred from a remote fixture's construction. It does not establish
that the synthetic distribution matches the NFL posterior or certify robustness.
Both deliberately wrong endpoints are isolated in 1,024/1,024 target-6 trials,
but only 701/1,024 target-7 trials (936 exclude at least one; 977 construct the
required sets). A failing joint vector cannot stand in for endpoint-specific
validation.

The analytic population-moment diagnostic is retained in
[its audit](audits/2026-11-19-population-moments.json).
It calculates unperturbed fine-target envelope moments using one-dimensional
quadrature, with independent absolute/linear/quadratic normal checks. Source review
identified that these are not the exact coarse-pilot population moments: the
coarse target includes a bounded .001 sine tilt. Clarification d267f8d precedes
complete tail results. The resulting mean-displacement bound is reported
separately and verified against a sine-tilted Gaussian identity; it is not a
covariance bound or a new numerical allowance. This does not sample a second
candidate or certify precision for an oracle proposal. Density ratios probed at
component centers are not global variance bounds. Differences from population
geometry also include the coarse-surrogate perturbation and finite-pilot shrinkage.
These reported theoretical bounds use quadrature-estimated moments; they do not
include a rigorous floating-point error bound. All coordinates here are latent
integration coordinates, not player expected points.

The initial-allocation calculation was declared at a660aba before complete tail
results. For target 7 the box within one component SD of (8,8), in its first two
coordinates, contains 4.66065% of target mass. With 2,048 pilot points in each
initial stratum, its expected point count is only .0312524; Markov's inequality
bounds the chance of any point there by 3.12524% under the declared Gaussian
point marginals. This does not assume independent Sobol points. Target 6's
corresponding expected count is 12.4178. These calculations concern the first
round only, not observed discovery frequency, second-round behavior or precision.

## Decision and next bounded milestone

Do not tune coefficients, covariance multipliers or seeds on this consumed study,
select plain results, automatically grow samples, change priors or relax limits.
The observed combination motivates one **mode-resolving proposal hypothesis**:
explicitly investigate discovery of separated mass and local functional coverage,
rather than only improving estimates of merged moments. Freeze support, independent
random domains, all search/fitting work, retained original/tail controls and a
stop rule before fresh experiments. Run a bounded feasibility preflight before
committing to another full certificate. This is a hypothesis, not an adopted fix.

The product milestone is still a defensibly validated cumulative contribution
module, with first-unit and both sensitivity benchmarks. Numerical success only
enables the unchanged archived pilot, then player calibration and observational
admission. Defense, participation and the cross-position objective remain required.
