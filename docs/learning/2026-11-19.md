## Concepts and failure analysis

Importance sampling is a way to spend computation where the integral needs it.
A full-support distribution can still spend too little work near a narrow remote
region. Matching a weighted mean and covariance also need not locate each mode of
a mixture. The separately declared population-moment diagnostic distinguishes
finite-pilot error from limitations of the proposed Gaussian components. Source
review caught that the calculation uses unperturbed fine-target envelopes, while
the pilot includes a bounded .001 sine tilt. The corrected labels and independent
Gaussian test accompany a separate mean-displacement bound; comparison gaps also
include this tilt and finite-pilot shrinkage. None is subtracted from errors. It does
not test or select a new oracle estimator. Probe-point density ratios are not
global precision bounds, and effective weights are not independent observations.

An independently declared initial-allocation calculation finds that a box holding
4.66% of the remote target's mass receives only .031 expected first-pilot points.
The chance of any point there is therefore at most 3.13% under Gaussian point
marginals, without assuming iid Sobol points. This explains an initial discovery
challenge; it is not a measured final failure or a reason to omit the case.

Three kinds of evidence must stay separate. Software tests verify formulas and
contracts. Known-answer trials measure the reference's numerical error and
precision in declared synthetic cases. Player calibration and attribution tests
ask whether the football model's uncertainty and allocation are defensible. A QB's
conditional estimate can be accurately computed while his individual contribution
remains confounded with protection, receivers or team context.

The broad-tail control now passes precision in all 1,024 trials. The first
displaced control improves from 0% to 33.59%, still below the required 90%.
All of its point discrepancies pass, but discrepancy plus numerical uncertainty
fails endpoint precision in 680 trials; eight also fail mean precision. The plain
comparison also fails at 40.23%. Its fitted geometry is close to the fine-target population moments,
so merely estimating those same moments with more pilot points is not an
evidence-backed fix. No sampled oracle comparison proves a family-level ceiling.
The remote target fails substantively: joint coverage is 30.96%, precision 0%,
and 47 confidence constructions fail. All 1,024 point estimates remain in the
error analysis; the second standardized mean's all-trial RMSE is .378 SD. Median
pilot mass stays near .9004 although the coarse target's total mass must lie in
[.999,1.001]. Its remote-center density-ratio probe barely improves. This is
consistent with missing a meaningful remote component, beyond insufficient
precision after fitting the intended moments. The initial stress diagnostic
exceeds the earlier NFL hierarchy concentration proxy in 8/32 trials; it does
not establish equivalent distributions or general robustness.
