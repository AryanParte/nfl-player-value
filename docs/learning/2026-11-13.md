## What the experiments establish

The design was committed before results (`aefe914`). Mathematical review found
that the proposed quadratic curved control has infinite Gaussian importance
variance. A pre-result amendment (`addf315`) replaces only that new control with
a bounded-sine transformation and independently known answers. No empirical
study trial ran on the earlier control. Accuracy limits and trial counts stay
fixed, and both plans remain visible.

All five known-answer gates pass: 5,120 reference trials finish, joint coverage is
94.43–96.39%, and every trial meets precision and rejects deliberately wrong
endpoints. This verifies the numerical-error construction on these functions;
it is not football uncertainty calibration or a universal coverage guarantee.
All 160 known-target density pairs and 19/20 archived pairs complete. The failed
pair remains in its requested denominator rather than disappearing from the study.

Stabilization improves held-out log score in every real-QB pair while reducing
training fit, supporting overfitting as one proposal problem. It is not uniformly
better: the simplest known target has a small deterioration, and individual
stress proposals still differ sharply. Coverage and the archived pilot always
use preselected pilot zero, never whichever fit later looks best.

The real-QB worst mean check improves from 0.030273 to 0.014228 posterior SD, still
above 0.01. The new check consists of 0.011703 point discrepancy plus 0.002525
numerical error. The reference is substantially more precise, but its point
discrepancy is larger. No confidence set establishes an error entirely outside
tolerance, so neither primary bias nor a passing result is demonstrated. The
team control passes. Hierarchy/fixed-year errors remain dominated by reference
imprecision; the prior-stress case fails during proposal construction.

The separately frozen trace reproduces that exception at offense precision
9.66e-24 in a matrix with scale about 6.96e6. Computed negative eigenvalues are
at roundoff scale. Positive model penalties do not guarantee that their tiny
contribution survives forming a floating-point normal equation. Arbitrary jitter
would change the specified model. The trace records and rethrows the failure;
it does not return zero likelihood or replace the original trial.

## Concepts and architectural decisions

A sampling proposal controls where the computer evaluates the posterior.
Dividing by its exact density preserves the target. Stabilizing proposal
covariances therefore changes computation, not the football prior or player
intervals. The weighted effective count measures concentration among dependent
integration points; it is not an independent sample count or participation rule.

Holdout density scores, numerical-error coverage, player calibration and
observational attribution answer different questions. A lower density second
moment can improve computation but cannot validate the QB/team allocation. The
same conditional likelihood code is shared by the primary/reference methods;
independent integration does not eliminate this shared-code limitation.

The Python offline/immutable-output architecture and existing React/TypeScript
stack are unchanged. No dependency or production scoring endpoint is added.
The new CLI requires complete verified known-answer evidence before its functional
pilot, and explicitly retains failures. The cumulative season ledger keeps every
archived completed game and negative estimate; eligibility remains independent
of the first-unit and sensitivity benchmark populations. Week 10 stays sealed.
