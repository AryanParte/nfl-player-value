## Statistical concepts and findings

**An observation is not an absence explanation.** Zero recorded dropbacks by the
previous QB differs from missing data, zero snaps, confirmed inactivity and a
verified injury. Weekly rosters resolve exact-game/team positions; they do not
identify why playing time changed. The result records that distinction, including
null comparisons when either QB has no training exposure. All real old-QB roster
records exist here, but that alone cannot certify their availability.

**Comparable cases are scarce.** From 96/154 adjacent pairs in the historical/
current windows, 61/112 pass the frozen rules. There are only eight/five QB changes
and four/two comparisons with both QBs represented in training. A missing trained
coefficient is not proof that the player's effect equals zero. Actor ambiguities
and split workloads exclude entire endpoints rather than quietly skipping them
and bridging to a more convenient game. That conservatism also creates selection
bias: results cannot be generalized to every difficult football circumstance.

**Target mix measures observed usage.** Total variation of recorded target shares
is zero when the mix is unchanged and one when the target sets do not overlap.
A distance of 0.5 means half the target-share mass moves. It does not establish
receiver quality, off-ball routes, protection or whether the QB created the
change. There are five/nine high-distance pairs with the same QB. Missing target
information stays null, not an unchanged cast.

**Prediction remains separate from attribution.** On changed-QB pairs, the joint
model's mean absolute change error exceeds the role baseline by 0.00254/0.00449
EPA per play in 2025/2026. High-target-mix results are mixed. Neither small
improvements elsewhere nor this negative comparison prove causal effects.
The leave-one-team-out ranges show sensitivity to particular offense teams;
overlapping games and shared opponents prevent interpreting them as confidence
intervals. These previously examined evaluation outcomes provide retrospective
diagnostics, not a fresh prospective holdout.

The isolated identity swap multiplies the new QB's observed dropbacks by the
frozen new-minus-prior coefficient difference. It holds targets, decisions and
opportunities fixed. This is a predecessor diagnostic, not the first-unit CAVAA
benchmark or an actual substitution. Mean team EPA changes separately reconcile
into context, team, shared-role, actor and residual changes; no player receives
all team EPA. Maximum reconstruction error is 1.20e-15 EPA per play. Negative
outcomes remain intact. No penalty, benchmark, eligibility threshold or admission
is changed, and no new CAVAA score is published.
