## Decisions and concepts

- Retrieval age and source age answer different questions. Downloading an old
  file today does not make its observations current. Embedded per-team timestamps
  also prevent one recent team update from hiding another team's stale chart.
- Full schedule inventory prevents an incomplete schedule file from making the
  observed PBP cohort appear complete. Every started-but-unreconciled game blocks
  the candidate, including in-progress games. This is conservative operational
  policy, not proof of independently verified NFL final status.
- Age limits are explicit, versioned project policy. They are not fitted player
  parameters or an nflverse service guarantee. Schedule/PBP reconciliation remains
  necessary even when timestamps are recent.
- Replay verifies the decision at its original cutoff. It does not renew freshness.
  A future publisher must reassess at its actual attempt time and bind that check
  to the proposed source set and last valid release.
- Corrections create new immutable versions. The comparison enumerates changed
  team-context rows only, not every player/name change or the cause of a change.
- Reproducibility is distinct from attribution. All CAVAA and defensive snap values
  stay null; all 17 modules remain unadmitted. No benchmark, qualification,
  regularization or uncertainty-calibration rule changed.
