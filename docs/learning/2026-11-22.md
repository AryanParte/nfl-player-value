## What was built and why

The [frozen design](../TAIL_MIXTURE_DESIGN.md), commit a3dd981, precedes producer
273e9ac and every new draw. The new proposal retains the existing bounded density
search, reserves a broad Gaussian where a duplicate leaves space, and fits two
independent importance-weighted mixture pilots. Component locations, covariances
and weights can represent mass without requiring a separate peak. The original
defensive component and exact weights preserve the target. No football prior,
likelihood, benchmark, cumulative accounting or admission definition changes.

This adapts the mixture-importance idea described by
[Cappé et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/0710.4242), using existing NumPy/SciPy.
We extend the existing frozen preflight runner rather than create a second
pipeline. Geometry, raw pilot responses, fitting steps, random domains, full work
and failure records are bound to immutable artifacts. A matched mode-only arm
recomputes identical initial fits but uses separate evaluation randomness.

## Statistical learning and actual failure analysis

A density-fitting objective is not the same as a mean or endpoint-integration
objective. Case 6 finds both modes in all trials. Its first mean RMSE improves
about elevenfold, but the first upper-endpoint RMSE worsens and 22/32 combined
endpoint checks fail. All point errors there remain within their tolerances:
numerical allowance must still fit inside the total accuracy requirement.

Case 7 has both problems. Nine trials miss a remote mode. Even among the 23
finding both, adaptation passes precision only ten times, versus 23 for mode-only.
Among the nine misses, adaptation passes once and baseline never does. These
groups explain behavior and cannot define a selectable population. All 32 point
estimates, including the two failed confidence constructions, remain in RMSE.
Global discovery alone is not a solution, and interval failures are not erased.

The candidate passes six small screens and fails four. This does not validate
95% coverage generally or establish NFL player attribution. No plain/baseline
fallback, extra samples, EM steps or changed tolerance was selected. See
[complete results](../TAIL_MIXTURE_RESULTS.md) for all cases and limitations.
