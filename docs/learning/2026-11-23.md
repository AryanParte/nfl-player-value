## What the experiment taught us

A density floor and accurate integration are different properties. Keeping half
the original proposal ensures q is at least half that density everywhere. It
bounds an unnormalized second moment when finite, but does not establish a
twofold variance bound, accurate ratio estimates or coverage for finite QMC
replicates with fitted controls. Splitting the fixed nets across more strata and
fitting more control columns also changes computational behavior.

The broad nonmodal case passes precision 32/32 versus mode-only 0/32 and tail-only
30/32. However, displaced case 6 passes 21/32 versus 32/32 and 11/32. Every trial
finds both modes. All individual point errors and allowances are within their
limits, but their sum exceeds the endpoint limit in 11 trials. More accurate
means do not guarantee better interval-endpoint estimates.

Remote case 7 has 12 missed-mode trials and 20 two-mode trials. Among the latter,
precision counts are protected 15, mode-only 20 and tail-only four. Global
discovery is not the only problem. Some remote RMSEs worsen despite improvement
in precision counts relative to tail-only; failures cannot be summarized solely
by favorable averages. One candidate and one mode-only confidence construction
fail at different trials. Both remain counted, with all defined point estimates.

All three remote arms have only 21/32 joint numerical coverage, a severe failure
of the fixed screen. Conversely, a small screen that passes does not certify
nominal coverage: case 0 passes with 28/32 joint coverage. Full coverage studies,
player uncertainty and observational attribution are separate requirements.
See [all cases and failure analysis](../PROTECTIVE_MIXTURE_RESULTS.md).
