## What changed and why

Design 4d5f6bb precedes implementation and fresh draws. For a normalized estimate
N/Z near anchor r, local numerical error depends on N-rZ. Separate numerator and
denominator variance reductions can lose useful covariance. The new two-direction
fit preserves that covariance algebraically, using independently fitted existing
zero-mean controls and a small fixed SVD ridge. Each prior keeps its own mass;
negative contributions and coefficients are never clipped. It changes numerical
integration, not the football model, benchmark or player interval.

Two analytically solvable Gaussian mixtures test broader tails and displaced
mass with a fixed defensive proposal. Independent normal/Student-t convolution
checks verify their endpoint probabilities. Every trial refits controls with
independent training and evaluation randomness. All original limits remain fixed;
the added targets were declared before testing and cannot be dropped afterward.

The existing runner, immutable stores, unittest suite and CI are reused. No new
library or service is introduced. The seven-case artifact contract cannot accept
an old five-case certificate. Raw fitting arrays reproduce the final coefficients,
and source/environment fingerprints prevent an old certificate from authorizing
changed numerical code.

## Results and failure analysis

All seven finite coverage checks pass, with joint coverage 95.1172%–96.0938%.
The smallest observed marginal coverage is 93.2617%; its p-value does not cross
the frozen family threshold. This finite result is not universal calibration.
Both full executions match the manifest and all 15 objects. Re-execution is not
an additional independent sample of 7,168 trials.

The broad-tail target passes all mean checks but fails endpoint precision in
962/1,024 trials. Its median maximum discrepancy plus numerical error is .001267
CDF against .001. Only two trials have point-only endpoint failures; numerical
uncertainty is the main obstacle. The plain estimator's precision pass rate is
also only 4.8828%, so this is not solely a control-fit failure.

Displaced mass fails both mean and endpoint precision in every trial. Point-only
failures occur in 734 and 811 trials; median combined errors are .0900 SD and
.01353 CDF. Every measured empirical variance is worse than plain. Training fit
improvement is therefore insufficient evidence for an integration improvement.
The maximum replicate mass share is 2.3205%, still below the prior NFL hierarchy
case's 10.3866%. This study does not certify that stronger regime.

Self-review identified two reporting risks. Point-error summaries must not omit
estimates simply because their confidence construction failed. Also, rejection
of a deliberately wrong joint vector can arise from an unrelated mean failure.
New analytical regressions guard both. The isolated diagnostic excludes both
wrong endpoints in all trials for targets 0–5, but only 421/1,024 for target 6;
at least one is excluded in every trial. The original gate remains unchanged.
None of these post-result diagnostics is an added acceptance criterion.

The saved failed certificate is actually rejected before `archive_inputs` is
called. No new NFL numerical pilot, player calibration, game inventory or score
publication follows. The archive remains November 7 evening, with Week 10 sealed.
