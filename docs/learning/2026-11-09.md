## What was built and why

The previous finite precision grid excluded some variance scales in failed
calibration controls. A frozen continuous log-normal prior removes that particular
support restriction while retaining the additive hierarchical model and source
cohort. Historical observations help estimate effects but add no 2026 points.
The candidate is still an observational QB dropback contrast, with coarse
team-season cast controls; it does not identify pure QB causality or measure
individual protection/receiver contributions.

The first numerical implementation failed, so a preregistered repair changed
sampling proposals and stratification while keeping the posterior and gates
fixed. Exact mixture-CDF root solving and spawned offline workers address measured
runtime costs. Source/manifests, benchmark weights, game ledgers, failed attempts
and unattempted denominators are checked explicitly. No new dependencies or
deployment infrastructure were added. Original attempts and frozen plans remain.

The initial full study ran too slowly. Several execution iterations were stopped
for measured bottlenecks; that is an execution cost, not scientific progress.
Before evaluating the remaining generators, the committed scope decision capped
execution at indices 0–7 without changing the 64 requested denominator or choosing
indices based on results. Full calibration remains unfinished. Future inference
work must establish both numerical reliability and a small runtime pilot before
launching a large campaign. This run should not be reported as a passed model.

## Actual results and the statistical lesson

The real QB fit has minimum ESS 76.17, below 128, and independent mean disagreement
0.03775 posterior SD, above 0.01. The team control fails nested mean convergence.
The prior sensitivities are also invalid. A single integration point carries
about 8.44% of one scramble's weight in a weak offense-variance tail. Finding the
same optimizer mode three times did not ensure reliable integration of that tail.

A **proposal** is where numerical samples are drawn; a **posterior** is the target
distribution defined by the data and model. Correct importance weights let us
change the former without choosing a more convenient scientific answer. Here
that repair still failed. Reproducibly computing the same approximation is also
different from accurately integrating the target.

The nominal 95% intervals cover 97.81% and 94.74% of synthetic truths among four
and three successful fits respectively. Those selected subsets cannot establish
calibration. All-requested coverage bounds are 6.11–99.86%, 4.44–99.75% and 0–100%.
Removing failed runs would create false confidence. Numerical failure prevents
interpreting these results as acceptance or statistical rejection of the prior.

Finally, exact additive accounting is not attribution validation. Maximum play
reconstruction error is 1.78e-15 EP and game-ledger error 2.22e-16 EP; QB/team
allocation still depends on hierarchical assumptions. A season effect distributed
across opportunities is not an independently observed weekly performance score.
No causal-identification gate was invented, and no observational gate was relaxed.
