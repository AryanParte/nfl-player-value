## Statistical concepts

An average depends on who enters it. The 3WR chart uses first-unit source ranks
1, 2 and 3 across three slots. Selecting only rank 1 would compare receivers with
each team's top-listed WR while omitting two other first-unit players. The adapter
retains all three; the existing benchmark contract splits each team-game role
cell's weight across its members. This is independent of actual targets, MVP
qualification, or how well a player performed.

A depth chart is a proxy for starting-caliber membership, not proof of talent or
participation. Formation context also matters: a 4-3 defensive end and a 3-4 end
map to different research roles. Those mappings do not establish pass-rush,
coverage or blocking assignments on individual plays. Missing identities and
hybrid roles remain structural uncertainty, not something a narrow model interval
can erase.

Time semantics matter too. A terminal end-game marker plus matching schedule
scores is evidence that a game finished. The later source retrieval tells us when
completion was observed; it does not reveal the exact final-whistle time. Similarly,
a September row in an October depth download does not independently prove what
was knowable in September. All current selections carry this retrospective label.

## Engineering decisions

Reused the existing bounded, content-addressed capture/replay layer for schedules;
no new third-party runtime dependency. The schedule's Eastern kickoff is converted
with daylight-saving-aware `zoneinfo`. Due games remain in the expected universe
when PBP is missing or completion evidence disagrees. The completion rule uses
cross-file corroboration, not an independent live final-status feed.

Select the latest whole-team pregame snapshot before judging its quality. Never
backfill an older favorable record or promote a backup when the expected starter
is missing. A provisional 72-hour freshness limit is recorded with the report.
Unknown formations, missing slots, identity conflicts and ambiguous roles block
cells. Nickel is an additional chart role, not a twelfth simultaneous defender;
a CB listed twice receives one membership. Fullback and holder are not silently
folded into RB or P.

The derived report has a separate immutable store and exact input receipt IDs.
`require_benchmark_inputs` checks the full game/team/role universe and requires
explicit retrospective acknowledgment before passing candidates to a model.
Diagnostics can be saved successfully with failures; they never advance a ranking
pointer, and `ranking_ready` remains false.
