## Decisions and concepts

- **Variance integration:** average complete posterior distributions over a
  declared finite variance prior, then invert the mixture CDF. Averaging interval
  endpoints or treating the best variance setting as certain is incorrect.
- **Historical information versus season value:** earlier seasons inform linked
  QB skill; they add zero 2026 opportunities or points. QB-year innovations allow
  changes, without discounting earlier 2026 games or multiplying current form
  backward over the season.
- **Matched-prior checks have a limited claim:** a simulation can verify the
  inference computation under its assumed generator while failing under another
  plausible generator. Avoiding the severe <90% flag is not a nominal-95% pass.
- **Known-ratio controls diagnose, not deploy:** improved oracle coverage points
  toward variance estimation/support, without proving that a new continuous-prior
  model will pass or selecting its real-data ratios.
- **Observation units matter:** 15,803 lineup rows and 262 weighted team-game
  means cover the same plays but provide different individual information and
  noise assumptions. Do not compare their marginal evidence as equivalent data.
