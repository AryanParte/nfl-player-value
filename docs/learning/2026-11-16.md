## What changed and why

The previous study located nearly all measured numerical error in coarse
integration. This unit therefore implements one fixed control-variate candidate,
committed before new trials. It subtracts patterns whose integrals are known to
be zero. A separate 4,096-point sample learns their coefficients; 64 independent
evaluation replicates then estimate the adjusted integral. Points within each
Sobol net are dependent; independence applies between fitting/evaluation stages
and between randomized replicates. The repaired fine-minus-
coarse correction remains, and the sum is normalized once.

The coefficients describe a numerical approximation, not football players.
They do not alter the football posterior, first-unit benchmark, two sensitivity
benchmarks, eligibility, priors or signed season ledger. Gaussian moment identities
justify the zero-mean controls; independent fitting avoids using evaluation noise
to choose the adjustment. Finite-sample numerical confidence still needs testing.

## Results, failure analysis and learning

All five analytic targets complete their 1,024 requested trials: 5,120 in total,
with joint numerical coverage 94.9219%–96.1914%, passing all marginal/joint and
precision gates. Every deliberately incorrect endpoint is rejected. Two full
network-disabled executions reproduce the same manifest and all 11 objects.
They replay the same trials; they are not 10,240 independent observations.

The important lesson is that regression fit and integration error have different
objectives. Mean training residual RMS falls to 17.6%–62.8% of response RMS, yet
20 of 35 measured integration variances increase. The ratios span 0.442–1.778.
Those quantities are correlated; this count is descriptive. A small fitting
residual is not a validation gate and does not guarantee better randomized
integration. The paired plain calculation is diagnostic, not a selectable fallback.

The real-QB's paired plain comparison is 0.013274 SD / 0.001136 CDF, nearly the
same and slightly better than the adjusted result. The smaller errors than the
previous run therefore cannot be credited to the method. For the worst primary
QB mean, 0.005554 SD point discrepancy plus 0.007758 SD numerical error exceeds
tolerance. All QB point discrepancies are individually within tolerance; no
confidence set excludes the agreement region. This is failed certification,
not proof of primary-estimate bias or justification for widening player intervals.

The team reference passes; all three consumed stress cases fail. Their mean-SD /
endpoint-CDF comparisons are 0.007482 / 0.002068, 0.053498 / 0.002998, and
0.014874 / 0.001107. Every point discrepancy alone meets tolerance, but numerical
uncertainty prevents certification; no confidence set excludes the agreement
region. All same-draw plain comparisons also fail except the team control. The
team model is not an individual contribution module. No
football test window is newly consumed. The archive still contains 57 QBs,
122 completed games and 8,996 season dropbacks through November 7 evening,
including 38 negative first-unit estimates. Week 10 remains sealed. Each case
uses 73,729 conditional evaluations including training. The longest takes
1,592.0 seconds and total case times sum to 4,531.4 / 4,531.9 seconds in the two
executions, inside 1,800/case and 7,200 total. These timings are separately
recorded in the [execution receipts](../audits/2026-11-16-coarse-executions.json).

The proposed next question is whether fitting against independent integration-
replicate means, rather than individual function evaluations, addresses the
observed mismatch within fixed work. Freeze matching net sizes, separate entropy,
budget and known-answer gates before new results. This is a hypothesis, not an
implemented solution or guaranteed pass. Do not add basis complexity blindly,
retry favorable seeds or add fine work to fix coarse error. The 192-fit player
calibration and observational admission remain separate unfinished milestones.
