The annual roster archive is a latest-record view, so it cannot supply weekly

The run also fixed `SeasonExposure`: a retrieval-based completion bound was being

to overlap. Completion now defaults to `observed_by`; only explicitly exact
finishes establish duration-based overlap. Duplicate games, equal kickoffs and
completion bounds beyond the cutoff still fail.

## Statistical concepts and decisions

An **observation** is not automatically an **exposure denominator**. A weekly
roster row may list a released, injured or practice-squad player. It does not prove
he played, was available at kickoff, or missed a game. Missing offensive workload
stays null; a measured zero target count within an observed workload stays zero.
Trades and position changes are matched by exact week without filling gaps or
appending both teams' entire seasons. A broad DL/DB/LB/OL label is insufficient to
identify the model's formation-specific role.

Two clocks are preserved: the game-cohort cutoff and the later evidence-retrieval
cutoff. New roster bytes cannot make old game coverage fresh or establish vintage
pregame knowledge. Historical use must acknowledge retrospective evidence. No
outcome is fitted or allocated here, no full EPA is copied to teammates, and this
join does not define the benchmark or qualify a player.

The existing stdlib capture/store/replay design was reused. No dataframe package,
database, service, secret or runtime dependency was added. Primary source positions
map only direct QB/RB/WR/TE/K/P/LS labels. `depth_chart_position` is retained as raw
evidence because the upstream dictionary warns it is not always accurate.
