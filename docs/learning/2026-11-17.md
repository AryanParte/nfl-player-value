## Statistical lesson and failure analysis

An estimate N/Z depends on both numerator and denominator. Its local error is
proportional to N−rZ. Reducing Var(N) alone can destroy useful cancellation with
Z. At the QB primary setting, upper-CDF numerator variance improves for all 171
training contrasts, while the fixed-anchor ratio-influence variance worsens for
164. All 171 worsen on evaluation. An analytical test obtains the exact variance
inflation 1+5r² when denominator noise is removed but numerator noise remains.

Mean precision is a separate blocker: median evaluation variance improves only
modestly and 15/171 mean contrasts worsen. The hierarchy stress case assigns
10.3866% of total mass to one replicate and fails badly even without the controls.
Its large numerical confidence sets do not prove either agreement or disagreement.
Neither issue is solved by the negligible fine correction, whose maximum
standard-error ratio to coarse error is 1.04e-9 across cases.

Next freeze one joint mass/numerator fit targeting normalized functional variance,
using existing composite controls, plus analytical tail-stress controls before
new draws. It must retain all current cases and mean/endpoint gates. This is a
proposal to test, not an adopted fix. No favorable-seed retries, interval widening,
plain-result fallback or adjustment of football priors follows from this failure.
