## Concepts and self-review

**Numerical precision** asks whether computation accurately represents the chosen
posterior. **Calibration** asks how its intervals behave under a specified data
generator. **Attribution** asks which player/team contrasts the assumptions can
support. A pass in one layer cannot substitute for another. These runs reuse
consumed synthetic stress cases; they provide no fresh calibration evidence.

The analytical controls caught two issues before NFL evaluation. Floating-point
Cholesky alone accepted singular geometry after scaling; a strict positive-
eigenvalue check now rejects it without a ridge. A strongly correlated known
target produced inaccurate QMC endpoints that the independent reference correctly
rejected. Separate one-dimensional Normal-plus-Student-t convolution roots verify
the correct endpoints. The test retains the rejection rather than weakening it.

benchmark/eligibility separation, exact weighted-node ordering, Jacobian and
