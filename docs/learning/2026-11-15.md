## What changed and why

The reference now combines a large, inexpensive coarse calculation with a smaller
paired correction from the repaired conditional solver. Both estimate unnormalized
integrals; their sum is normalized once. Subtracting separately normalized answers
would break that identity. Fine and coarse correction evaluations use identical
coordinates, while the large coarse net is independent. Saved replicate vectors
retain normalizing-mass covariance and signed contributions.

This changes how we measure numerical integration error, not the football
posterior, player intervals, first-unit benchmark, sensitivities, eligibility or
season ledger. Existing model and prediction objects remain unchanged.
